Optomechanics · Quantum technologies · Fundamental physics

Controlling light and motion at the quantum limit

We are a group of researchers from the Physics and Astronomy department of the University of Florence and the National Institute of Optics (CNR-INO), based in Sesto Fiorentino. We study how light and mechanical motion interact in microfabricated oscillators and levitated nanoparticles, to explore quantum phenomena in macroscopic objects, to build quantum sensors, and to test fundamental physics, including gravity.

What we do

Clamped micro- and nano-fabricated devices

Silicon-nitride membranes and micro-oscillating mirrors in optical cavities, for squeezing and quantum-limited measurement.

Levitated optomechanics

Nanoparticles levitated in optical tweezers and cooled to the quantum regime of their motion.

Foundations, with INFN

We are always happy to hear from people who share our interest in optomechanics and quantum physics.

Join the group or visit us

We currently have no funded postdoctoral or PhD positions. If you would like to join the team, for example by applying together for a fellowship or a grant, please get in touch and tell us about your background and interests.

If you would like to visit the group, for a few days or a longer stay, write to us and we will see what is possible.

Master students

If our research topics interest you, we are always happy to discuss possible dissertation projects. Get in touch and we will talk about what is available.

Clamped micro- and nano-fabricated devices

Scanning electron microscope (SEM) images of the mechanical devices we have developed over the years. These are realized on SOI silicon wafers with an embedded high-reflectivity coating for 1064 nm light. The coating is usually the whitest region in the images. The devices were designed by our collaborators at IMEM-CNR and TIFPA in Trento and fabricated at TU Delft in the Netherlands.
